Questions

Can I use this to count days between two dates?

Yes. Put the earlier date first and the later date second.

How age is calculated

Age is the number of full years between the date of birth and today. Subtract the birth year from the current year, then take one off if this year's birthday has not happened yet.

Worked example

Someone born on 15 August 1990 is 34 on 1 June 2025, because the birthday in August has not arrived, and turns 35 on 15 August 2025.

Months and days

Months vary from 28 to 31 days, so an exact age counts whole years first, then whole months, then the days left over.

Leap day birthdays

People born on 29 February have a real birthday only every four years. In other years the legal birthday is 28 February or 1 March, depending on the country.
