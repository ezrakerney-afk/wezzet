Power converter

Convert between every common unit of power. Type a value and pick units.

About power units

Power is the rate of using or producing energy. One watt is one joule per second; a mechanical horsepower is about 745.7 W and a metric horsepower about 735.5 W.

This converter handles 12 power units: milliwatts, watts, kilowatts, megawatts, gigawatts, horsepower, brake horsepower, metric horsepower, btu per hour, tons of refrigeration, joules per second, dbm. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
