Text compare

Questions

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The comparison runs in your browser.

How big can the texts be?

Tens of thousands of lines work fine on most devices.

What do the colors mean?

Green lines were added, red lines were removed. Bold parts show the exact words that changed.

How text comparison works

A diff shows what changed between two versions of a text. It finds the longest sequence of lines the two share and marks everything else as removed from the old version or added to the new one.

Reading the result

Lines marked with a minus, usually red, exist only in the first text. Lines marked with a plus, usually green, exist only in the second. A changed line appears as a removal followed by an addition.

Tips

Differences in spaces, tabs or line endings count as changes, so normalize them if only the words matter. Sort lists before comparing when the order is unimportant.

Uses

Reviewing edits to contracts and essays, checking what changed in a configuration file, and comparing two exports of the same data.
