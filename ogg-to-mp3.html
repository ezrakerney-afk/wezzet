    Questions

    Is my video uploaded?

    No. The converter runs on your device. The first time, your browser downloads the converter itself (about 32 MB); your files never leave.

    Why is it slow on big videos?

    It runs on your computer's processor inside the browser, which is slower than a desktop app. Short clips and audio are quick.
