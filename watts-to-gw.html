How to convert watts to gigawatts

One watt equals 1e-9 gigawatts. To convert, multiply the number of watts by 1e-9:

gigawatts = watts × 1e-9

To go the other way, divide by 1e-9, or multiply by 1,000,000,000: watts = gigawatts × 1,000,000,000.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 watts. Multiply 7.5 by 1e-9: 7.5 × 1e-9 = 7.5e-9 gigawatts.
