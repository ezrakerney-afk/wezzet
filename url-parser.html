Questions

Is the URL sent anywhere?

No. It is parsed in your browser.

Are values decoded?

Yes. %%20 becomes a space and + in the query becomes a space, so you can read the real values.

What if I leave out https://?

It is added for you, so example.com/page works.

The parts of a URL

Take https://user@example.com:8080/path/page?id=5&sort=asc#top.

The scheme (https) is the protocol. The host (example.com) is the server, optionally with a port (8080); the defaults are 443 for https and 80 for http. The path (/path/page) names the resource. The query after the ? is a list of key=value pairs joined by &. The fragment after the # points to a spot in the page and is never sent to the server.

Encoding

Characters that are not allowed in a URL are percent-encoded, so a space becomes %20.

Tracking parameters

Query keys such as utm_source and fbclid only record where a visitor came from. They can be removed without changing the page that loads.
