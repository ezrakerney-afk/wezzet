    Questions

    How do I convert PES to DST?

    Drop your PES file in the hoop. DST is already picked, so press Download.

    Are thread colors kept?

    DST doesn't store thread colors, so note them from the swatches before you stitch.

    Is my design uploaded?

    No. The file is converted in your browser.

    About PES and DST

    What is PES?

    PES is the embroidery format for Brother, Babylock and Bernina home machines. It stores stitches together with thread color information.

    What is DST?

    DST is the Tajima embroidery format and the closest thing to an industry standard for commercial machines. It stores stitch movements only, with no thread colors.

    When to convert PES to DST

    Choose DST for commercial and multi-needle machines. Colors are not saved, so keep a note of the thread order.

    How to convert

    Choose your PES file or drop it on the page, check the output setting, and download the DST result. You can add several files at once.
