About lorem ipsum

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used to show how a layout will look before the real words exist. Because it resembles ordinary text in rhythm but means nothing, viewers look at the design and not the wording.

Where it comes from

It is scrambled Latin from Cicero's work De finibus bonorum et malorum, written in 45 BC. The opening words come from a passage beginning with dolorem ipsum, meaning pain itself. Printers have used it since at least the 1500s, and it became common in the 1960s with Letraset sheets and later with desktop publishing software.

When not to use it

Replace it before launch, and use real draft copy where the length of the text affects the design, such as buttons, headings and error messages.
