How to convert coulombs to amp hours

One coulomb equals 0.0002777778 amp hours. To convert, multiply the number of coulombs by 0.0002777778:

amp hours = coulombs × 0.0002777778

To go the other way, divide by 0.0002777778, or multiply by 3,600: coulombs = amp hours × 3,600.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 coulombs. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0002777778: 7.5 × 0.0002777778 = 0.0020833333 amp hours.
