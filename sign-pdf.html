Is this a legal e-signature?

It adds a picture of your signature, like signing a printout. That's enough for many forms, but some contracts need a certified signing service.

About signing PDFs

An electronic signature is a mark you add to a document to show you agree to it, such as a drawn or typed signature placed on the page. In the United States (ESIGN Act and UETA), the European Union (eIDAS) and many other places, electronic signatures are legally valid for most agreements.

Electronic versus digital

A digital signature is a stricter kind that uses a certificate to prove who signed and that the file has not changed since. Some documents require one, or require a handwritten signature or a notary, such as wills and certain property and court papers. Check what the recipient accepts.

Tips

Read the whole document first, sign and date where indicated, and keep a copy of the signed file.
