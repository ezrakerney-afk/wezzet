Signature

Click a page to place your signature. Drag to move it, drag the corner to resize.

Questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF and your signature stay in your browser.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Click each page where you want the signature.

Is this a legal e-signature?

It adds a picture of your signature, like signing a printout. That's enough for many forms, but some contracts need a certified signing service.
