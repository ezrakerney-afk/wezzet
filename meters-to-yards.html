Meters to Yards

Convert meters to yards, with a table of every other length unit.

How to convert meters to yards

One meter equals 1.093613298 yards. To convert, multiply the number of meters by 1.093613298:

yards = meters × 1.093613298

To go the other way, divide by 1.093613298, or multiply by 0.9144: meters = yards × 0.9144.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 meters. Multiply 7.5 by 1.093613298: 7.5 × 1.093613298 = 8.202099738 yards.
