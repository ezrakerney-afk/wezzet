How to convert bar to atmospheres

One bar equals 0.9869232667 atmospheres. To convert, multiply the number of bar by 0.9869232667:

atmospheres = bar × 0.9869232667

To go the other way, divide by 0.9869232667, or multiply by 1.01325: bar = atmospheres × 1.01325.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 bar. Multiply 7.5 by 0.9869232667: 7.5 × 0.9869232667 = 7.4019245 atmospheres.
