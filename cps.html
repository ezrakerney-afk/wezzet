About click speed

Clicks per second (CPS) is the number of clicks divided by the length of the test. 65 clicks in 10 seconds is 6.5 CPS.

Typical scores

Most people click 6 to 7 times a second with ordinary clicking. Practiced players reach 9 to 12, and special techniques go higher.

Techniques

Jitter clicking tenses the arm to vibrate the finger. Butterfly clicking alternates two fingers on one button. Drag clicking slides a finger across the button so friction fires many clicks.

A caution

Fast clicking strains the hand and forearm, so stop if you feel pain. Many game servers also forbid very high click rates or double-click mice, so check the rules before relying on them.
