How to convert megapascals to psi

One megapascal equals 145.0377377 psi. To convert, multiply the number of megapascals by 145.0377377:

psi = megapascals × 145.0377377

To go the other way, divide by 145.0377377, or multiply by 0.0068947573: megapascals = psi × 0.0068947573.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 megapascals. Multiply 7.5 by 145.0377377: 7.5 × 145.0377377 = 1,087.783033 psi.
