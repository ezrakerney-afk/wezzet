    About MOV and MP3

    What is MOV?

    MOV is Apple's QuickTime container and the format iPhones and many cameras record in. It is closely related to MP4 but is less reliably supported on Windows, Android and the web.

    What is MP3?

    MP3 is the most compatible audio format there is. It is lossy: it removes sound most people cannot hear to shrink files to roughly a tenth of CD size at 128 to 192 kbps.

    When to convert MOV to MP3

    Choose MP3 when the audio must play on anything: car stereos, old players, phones and every app.
