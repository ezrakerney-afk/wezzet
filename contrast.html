Questions

What ratio do I need?

WCAG AA asks for 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (24px, or 19px bold). AAA asks for 7:1 and 4.5:1.

What color formats work?

Hex like #333 or #1a73e8, and rgb(26, 115, 232).

Can I share a result?

Yes. The colors are saved in the page link, so copy the address bar.

About color contrast

Contrast ratio compares the brightness of text and its background, from 1:1 (identical) to 21:1 (black on white).

WCAG requirements

Level AA, the usual legal and design standard, needs at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (about 24 px, or 19 px bold) and for interface parts such as icons and input borders. Level AAA needs 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.

How it is calculated

(L1 + 0.05) ÷ (L2 + 0.05), where L1 and L2 are the relative luminance of the lighter and darker colors.

Why it matters

Low contrast is hard to read for people with low vision, on dim screens and in sunlight. Also avoid relying on color alone to carry meaning: about 1 in 12 men has some form of color blindness.
