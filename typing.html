How typing speed is measured

Typing speed is given in words per minute (WPM), where a word is counted as five characters including spaces. Typing 250 characters in one minute is 50 WPM.

Accuracy

Net WPM subtracts mistakes, so it is the figure that matters. Accuracy is the share of correct characters; aim for 95% or better before pushing speed.

What is a good speed?

The average adult types about 40 WPM. Around 60 to 80 WPM is comfortable for office and writing work, and professional typists exceed 100.

How to improve

Learn touch typing with your fingers resting on the home row (ASDF and JKL;), keep your eyes on the screen, and practice a few minutes a day. Accuracy first; speed follows.
