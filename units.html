Questions

Are these conversions exact?

They use the official definitions (for example 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly) and are shown to 6 significant figures.

About unit conversion

Converting a measurement means multiplying by the ratio between two units. To go from inches to centimeters, multiply by 2.54, because one inch is defined as exactly 2.54 cm.

Metric and US customary

The metric system (SI) steps in powers of ten: 1,000 meters in a kilometer, 1,000 grams in a kilogram. US customary and imperial units use older ratios, such as 12 inches in a foot and 16 ounces in a pound. Since 1959 these have been defined exactly in metric terms, so conversions are precise.

Units that share a name

A US gallon (3.785 L) is smaller than an imperial gallon (4.546 L), and a US ton (2,000 lb) is lighter than a metric tonne (1,000 kg). Check which one a figure uses.

Temperature

Temperature scales have different zero points, so they need a formula, not a single factor.
