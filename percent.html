Questions

How do I work out percentage change?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.

How to calculate percentages

Percent means per hundred. Three questions cover almost every case.

What is X% of Y?

Multiply: Y × X ÷ 100. 15% of 80 is 80 × 0.15 = 12.

X is what percent of Y?

Divide and multiply by 100: X ÷ Y × 100. 12 out of 80 is 15%.

Percentage change

(new − old) ÷ old × 100. A price going from 80 to 92 has risen by 12 ÷ 80 × 100 = 15%.

Common traps

A 20% rise followed by a 20% fall does not return to the start: 100 becomes 120, then 96. And a change from 10% to 12% is two percentage points, but a 20 percent increase.
