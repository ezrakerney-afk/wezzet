Quarts to pounds

Convert quarts to pounds (assumes water, 1 g per mL), with a table of every other water weight unit.

How to convert quarts to pounds

One quart equals 2.086351113 pounds. To convert, multiply the number of quarts by 2.086351113:

pounds = quarts × 2.086351113

To go the other way, divide by 2.086351113, or multiply by 0.4793057093: quarts = pounds × 0.4793057093.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 quarts. Multiply 7.5 by 2.086351113: 7.5 × 2.086351113 = 15.64763335 pounds.
