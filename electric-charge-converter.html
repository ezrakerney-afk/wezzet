Electric charge converter

Convert between every common unit of electric charge. Type a value and pick units.

About electric charge units

Electric charge is measured in coulombs; one coulomb is one ampere flowing for one second. Batteries are rated in ampere-hours, and 1 Ah equals 3,600 C.

This converter handles 8 electric charge units: picocoulombs, nanocoulombs, microcoulombs, millicoulombs, coulombs, electron charges, milliamp hours, amp hours. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
