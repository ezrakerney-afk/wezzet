A few useful things

Draw with a mouse, finger or stylus. Pressure affects brush size when your stylus and browser support it.

Touch: use two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom. Turn on “Draw with pen only” for finger panning while drawing with a stylus.

Keyboard: B brush · E eraser · G fill · I color picker · V move layer · H pan · L line · R rectangle · O ellipse · T text. Space + drag pans. Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes, Shift Z redoes, S saves.

Saving: a recovery copy is saved in this browser after edits when storage is available. Download a project for a permanent copy. Recovery can be removed by your browser; private browsing may not keep it.

Layers: up to 12 layers. Undo keeps up to 30 changes within a 48 MB memory budget; older changes drop off as needed. Text and shapes become pixels once placed.
