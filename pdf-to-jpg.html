    Questions

    Which resolution should I pick?

    Screen is fine for sharing and slides. 150 dpi is sharp on most screens. Use 300 dpi if you're printing.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Pages are drawn in your browser with the open-source pdf.js library.
