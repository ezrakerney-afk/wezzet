    Questions

    Can I get the sound from a video?

    Yes. Drop in an MP4, MOV or WebM video and you get just the audio as an MP3.

    Which quality should I pick?

    192 kbps sounds good for almost everything. Use 128 for speech and small files, 320 for music you care about.

    Why won't my file open?

    Your browser decodes the audio, so a few formats depend on the browser. Chrome and Edge open the most. Very long videos can run out of memory.

    Is my file uploaded?

    No. Everything happens on your device with the open-source LAME encoder.

    About MP3 and bitrates

    MP3 shrinks audio by removing sound that most listeners cannot hear. How much is removed is set by the bitrate, in kilobits per second.

    Choosing a bitrate

    128 kbps is acceptable for speech and casual listening, at about 1 MB a minute. 192 kbps is a good middle ground for music. 320 kbps is the maximum, at about 2.4 MB a minute, and is very hard to tell apart from a CD. For spoken word, 64 to 96 kbps in mono is enough.

    VBR and CBR

    Variable bitrate uses more data for complex passages and less for simple ones, giving better quality for the size. Constant bitrate is more predictable for streaming and old players.

    Converting a low-bitrate file to a higher bitrate does not improve it; the lost detail is gone. Always convert from the best source you have.
