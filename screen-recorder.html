Questions

Is there a time limit or watermark?

No. Recording length is only limited by your device's memory, and there is no watermark.

What format is the video?

WebM in most browsers, MP4 in Safari. Use the WebM to MP4 converter if you need MP4.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. It is recorded and saved on your device.

About screen recording

A screen recording captures what is on your display as video, optionally with sound and your voice.

What you can capture

Browsers let you choose the whole screen, one window or one browser tab. Sharing a tab is the most reliable way to include that tab's audio. System-wide audio capture depends on the browser and operating system.

Tips

Close or hide anything private first, including notifications, bookmarks and other tabs. Record at the size you will share at. Do a ten-second test to check the sound before a long take.

File size

Recordings are usually saved as WebM or MP4. An hour of 1080p screen video is commonly several hundred megabytes, depending on how much moves on screen.

Ask before recording other people in calls; in many places consent is required by law.
