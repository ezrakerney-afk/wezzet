    Questions

    What does Keep shape do?

    It stops the photo from stretching. Fill in just the width or just the height and the other side is worked out for you. Fill in both and the image fits inside that box.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Resizing happens in your browser.

    Can I make an image bigger?

    Yes, but it won't gain detail. Big jumps can look soft.
