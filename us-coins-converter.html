About us coins units

US coins are the penny (1 cent), nickel (5), dime (10), quarter (25), half dollar (50) and dollar coin (100 cents).

This converter handles 7 us coins units: pennies, cents, nickels, dimes, quarters, half dollars, dollars. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
