How to convert kiloelectron volts to electron volts

One kiloelectron volt equals 1,000 electron volts. To convert, multiply the number of kiloelectron volts by 1,000:

electron volts = kiloelectron volts × 1,000

To go the other way, divide by 1,000, or multiply by 0.001: kiloelectron volts = electron volts × 0.001.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 kiloelectron volts. Multiply 7.5 by 1,000: 7.5 × 1,000 = 7,500 electron volts.
