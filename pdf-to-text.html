Questions

Why is the text empty?

The PDF is probably a scan, so its pages are pictures with no text in them.

Does it keep the layout?

It keeps lines and pages in order. Columns, tables and fonts are not kept.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The text is read in your browser with the open-source pdf.js library. Summarize with AI sends only the text, and only when you click it.

About PDF and text

What is PDF?

PDF keeps a document's layout fixed, so it looks the same on every screen and printer.

What is text?

Plain text is characters only, with no formatting, and opens in any editor.

When to convert PDF to text

Choose plain text to read, search or paste the content anywhere without formatting getting in the way.

How to convert

Choose your PDF file or drop it on the page, check the output setting, and download the text result. You can add several files at once.
