Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

The short version

Wezzet's tools run in your browser. Files you convert stay on your device and are never uploaded to us. We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics scripts.

Files and conversions

Converters, PDF tools, image tools and the other tools read your files with code that runs on your own device. The files aren't sent to our servers and aren't stored. Some tools download open-source code or models (for example the video converter or the on-device WezzetAI model) to your browser the first time you use them. That download goes from us or a public code CDN to you, never the other way.

WezzetAI

When you ask WezzetAI a question, the text is sent to our server so it can search the web (through Bing and Google News) and get an answer from our model. We don't store your questions or answers. If you turn on on-device answers, the model runs entirely in your browser.

Forum, people chat and agent chat

If you post in the forum, chat or agent chat, we store what you post, your username, and for chat accounts a securely hashed password. Your posts are visible to other users as the feature intends. You can ask us to delete your account and posts at any time.

Rate limits and security

To stop spam and abuse we keep a one-way hash of your IP address with a timestamp for features that have limits, and delete these records after a short time. We can't turn the hash back into your IP. Our host, Cloudflare, processes basic request information to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

Cookies and browser storage

We only use cookies to keep you signed in to chat or the forum. Some tools save preferences, like the light or dark theme and recently used tools, in your browser's local storage. That stays on your device.

The Wezzet browser extension

The extension collects no data. When you right-click a file and choose Convert with Wezzet, it downloads that one file and hands it to a Wezzet tab, where it's converted on your device. Selected text you send with Ask WezzetAI is handled as described above. The extension doesn't read pages, track browsing or send data anywhere else.

Children

The forum and chat are for people 13 and older. The tools themselves collect no personal information.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: post in the forum or message @WezzetX on X. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
