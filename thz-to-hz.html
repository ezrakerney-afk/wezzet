How to convert terahertz to hertz

One terahertz equals 1,000,000,000,000 hertz. To convert, multiply the number of terahertz by 1,000,000,000,000:

hertz = terahertz × 1,000,000,000,000

To go the other way, divide by 1,000,000,000,000, or multiply by 1e-12: terahertz = hertz × 1e-12.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 terahertz. Multiply 7.5 by 1,000,000,000,000: 7.5 × 1,000,000,000,000 = 7,500,000,000,000 hertz.
