Convert seconds to minutes, with a table of every other time unit.

How to convert seconds to minutes

One second equals 0.0166666667 minutes. To convert, multiply the number of seconds by 0.0166666667:

minutes = seconds × 0.0166666667

To go the other way, divide by 0.0166666667, or multiply by 60: seconds = minutes × 60.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 seconds. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0166666667: 7.5 × 0.0166666667 = 0.125 minutes.
