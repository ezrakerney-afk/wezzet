How loan payments are calculated

A fixed-rate loan is repaid in equal monthly payments worked out with the amortization formula:

payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n)

P is the amount borrowed, r is the monthly interest rate (the annual rate divided by 12) and n is the number of monthly payments.

Worked example

Borrowing 20,000 at 6% a year for 5 years: r = 0.005 and n = 60, giving a payment of 386.66 a month. Over the term you repay 23,199.36, of which 3,199.36 is interest.

How the balance falls

Early payments are mostly interest and later ones mostly principal. Extra payments go straight to principal, which shortens the loan and cuts the total interest.

APR

The annual percentage rate includes fees as well as interest, so compare loans by APR. This calculator gives estimates; your lender's figures are the ones that apply.
