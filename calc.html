    About calculator order of operations

    Calculations follow a fixed order: parentheses first, then exponents, then multiplication and division from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right.

    Worked example

    2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20, because multiplication comes first. Write (2 + 3) × 4 to get 20.

    Percent

    15% means 0.15, so 200 × 15% is 30.

    Scientific functions

    Check whether trigonometry is set to degrees or radians: sin(90) is 1 in degrees but about 0.894 in radians.

    Decimal rounding

    Computers store numbers in binary, so some decimals are not exact: 0.1 + 0.2 gives 0.30000000000000004 in raw floating point. For money, round to two decimal places at the end.
