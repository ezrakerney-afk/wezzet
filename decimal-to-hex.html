Decimal to hex converter

Convert decimal numbers to hex, including fractions and negative numbers.

How to convert decimal to hex

Divide the decimal number by 16 over and over, writing down each remainder. Read the remainders from last to first.

Worked example: 156 to hex

156 ÷ 16 = 9 remainder C
9 ÷ 16 = 0 remainder 9

Reading the remainders bottom to top gives 9C.
