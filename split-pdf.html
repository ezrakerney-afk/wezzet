Questions

What do the options do?

Extract saves the pages you list as one new PDF, in the order you typed them. Split saves each page as its own PDF in a zip. Delete removes those pages. Rotate turns them.

How do I write the pages?

Use commas and dashes: 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, page 5, and page 8 to the end. Leave it empty for every page.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
