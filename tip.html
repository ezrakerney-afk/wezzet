How to work out a tip

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage as a decimal, then add it to the bill: tip = bill × percent ÷ 100.

Worked example

A 48.00 bill with an 18% tip: 48 × 0.18 = 8.64, so the total is 56.64. Split between 3 people, that is 18.88 each.

Quick mental math

Ten percent is the bill with the decimal point moved one place left (4.80). Double it for 20% (9.60), or add half again for 15% (7.20).

How much to tip

In the United States, 15 to 20 percent is usual for table service, and many people tip on the amount before tax. In much of Europe and Asia service is included or a small round-up is enough, so check local custom when you travel.
