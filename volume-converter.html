Volume converter

Convert between every common unit of volume. Type a value and pick units.

About volume units

Volume measures capacity. The liter is a cubic decimeter; a US gallon is exactly 3.785411784 L while an imperial gallon is 4.54609 L, so US and UK pints, quarts and gallons are not interchangeable.

This converter handles 16 volume units: centiliters, microliters, milliliters, liters, cubic meters, cubic centimeters, cubic inches, cubic feet, teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons, imperial gallons. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
