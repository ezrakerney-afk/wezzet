Volts to Microvolts

Convert volts to microvolts, with a table of every other voltage unit.

How to convert volts to microvolts

One volt equals 1,000,000 microvolts. To convert, multiply the number of volts by 1,000,000:

microvolts = volts × 1,000,000

To go the other way, divide by 1,000,000, or multiply by 0.000001: volts = microvolts × 0.000001.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 volts. Multiply 7.5 by 1,000,000: 7.5 × 1,000,000 = 7,500,000 microvolts.
