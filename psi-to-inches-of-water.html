How to convert psi to inches of water

One psi equals 27.67990582 inches of water. To convert, multiply the number of psi by 27.67990582:

inches of water = psi × 27.67990582

To go the other way, divide by 27.67990582, or multiply by 0.0361272906: psi = inches of water × 0.0361272906.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 psi. Multiply 7.5 by 27.67990582: 7.5 × 27.67990582 = 207.5992937 inches of water.
