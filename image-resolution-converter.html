Image resolution converter

Convert between every common unit of image resolution. Type a value and pick units.

About image resolution units

Image resolution is how many dots or pixels fit in a length. Print uses dots per inch (300 dpi for photo-quality prints) and screens use pixels per inch or per centimeter.

This converter handles 5 image resolution units: dots per inch, pixels per inch, dots per centimeter, dots per meter, dots per millimeter. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.

Accuracy

Factors are the exact defined values where a definition exists, and results show up to 10 significant digits. Round to the precision of your own measurement.
