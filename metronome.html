Questions

How do I use tap tempo?

Tap the button in time with the music, 4 taps or more. The BPM is set from the average gap between taps.

Is the timing accurate?

Yes. Clicks are scheduled with the Web Audio clock, so they stay steady even if the page is busy.

Is there a shortcut?

Press the space bar to start and stop.

About tempo and metronomes

A metronome clicks at a steady tempo measured in beats per minute (BPM). At 60 BPM there is one beat a second; at 120 there are two.

Tempo markings

Largo is about 40 to 60 BPM, adagio 66 to 76, andante 76 to 108, moderato 108 to 120, allegro 120 to 156 and presto 168 to 200.

Time signatures

In 4/4 there are four beats in a bar with the first accented; in 3/4, the waltz pattern, there are three.

How to practice

Start slower than you think you need, where you can play the passage without mistakes. Raise the tempo by about 5 BPM at a time. To build your own sense of time, set the click to half speed so it sounds only on beats 2 and 4.
