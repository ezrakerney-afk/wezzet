Questions

How do I use tap tempo?

Tap the button in time with the music, 4 taps or more. The BPM is set from the average gap between taps.

Is the timing accurate?

Yes. Clicks are scheduled with the Web Audio clock, so they stay steady even if the page is busy.

Is there a shortcut?

Press the space bar to start and stop.
