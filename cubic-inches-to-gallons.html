How to convert cubic inches to gallons

One cubic inche equals 0.0043290043 gallons. To convert, multiply the number of cubic inches by 0.0043290043:

gallons = cubic inches × 0.0043290043

To go the other way, divide by 0.0043290043, or multiply by 231: cubic inches = gallons × 231.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 cubic inches. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0043290043: 7.5 × 0.0043290043 = 0.0324675325 gallons.
