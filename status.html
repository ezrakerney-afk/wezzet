    Questions

    What do the status codes mean?

    2xx is success, 3xx is a redirect, 4xx means the page was not found or is blocked (404, 403) and 5xx is a server error.

    Why does my site show 403 here but work in my browser?

    Some sites block automated checks or visitors from data centers. The check runs from Cloudflare, not from your computer.

    How many redirects are followed?

    Up to 10, so you can spot redirect loops and long chains that slow pages down.

    Is there an API?

    Yes: wezzet.com/api/status?url=example.com returns the chain and headers as text or JSON.
