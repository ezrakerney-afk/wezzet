How to convert inches to centimeters

One inche equals 2.54 centimeters. To convert, multiply the number of inches by 2.54:

centimeters = inches × 2.54

To go the other way, divide by 2.54, or multiply by 0.3937007874: inches = centimeters × 0.3937007874.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 inches. Multiply 7.5 by 2.54: 7.5 × 2.54 = 19.05 centimeters.
