WEZZET / CREATE & EDIT

Transcription & subtitles

Turn English audio or video into an editable transcript and timestamped SRT or VTT subtitles, on your device.

Uses a small English Whisper model. Model files download on first use; media is not uploaded. Long recordings are processed in sections. Accuracy varies: review names, silence and timestamps before sharing. Desktop recommended; keep this tab open.

Text edits affect the TXT download. Edit timestamped captions below to update subtitle downloads. Format: one cue per line, start seconds | end seconds | text.

Up to 250 MB and two hours per file. Browser memory limits still apply. Video and audio processing downloads a 32 MB engine the first time; transcription also downloads an AI model. Long files can take a while.
