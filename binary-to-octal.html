Binary to octal converter

Convert binary numbers to octal, including fractions and negative numbers.

How to convert binary to octal

Group the binary digits in sets of 3 starting from the right (pad the left with zeros), then replace each group with one octal digit.

Worked example: 10011100 to octal

Split 10011100 into groups of three, padding the left: 010, 011 and 100. Those are 2, 3 and 4, giving 234 in octal. Both equal 156 in decimal.

Where this comes up

Binary is how computers store everything: each digit (bit) is one on or off state. Octal is compact and maps cleanly onto groups of three bits.
