Hex to octal converter

Convert hex numbers to octal, including fractions and negative numbers.

How to convert hex to octal

Go through binary: write each hex digit as 4 bits, then regroup the bits in sets of 3 from the right and read each group as one octal digit.

Worked example: 9C to octal

9C in hex is 10011100 in binary, which regroups to 234 in octal. Both equal 156 in decimal.

Where this comes up

Hex packs four bits per digit, so one byte is always two hex digits. It is used for colors (#FF8800), memory addresses and byte dumps. Octal is compact and maps cleanly onto groups of three bits.
