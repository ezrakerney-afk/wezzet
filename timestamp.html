Questions

What is a Unix timestamp?

The number of seconds since 1 January 1970 00:00 UTC. It is also called epoch time or POSIX time.

Seconds or milliseconds?

Both work. Up to 11 digits is read as seconds, 12 to 14 digits as milliseconds (what JavaScript uses), longer as micro or nanoseconds.

What is the year 2038 problem?

Systems that store the timestamp in a signed 32-bit number overflow on 19 January 2038.
