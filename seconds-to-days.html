Seconds to days

Convert seconds to days, with a table of every other time unit.

How to convert seconds to days

One second equals 0.0000115741 days. To convert, multiply the number of seconds by 0.0000115741:

days = seconds × 0.0000115741

To go the other way, divide by 0.0000115741, or multiply by 86,400: seconds = days × 86,400.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 seconds. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0000115741: 7.5 × 0.0000115741 = 0.0000868056 days.
