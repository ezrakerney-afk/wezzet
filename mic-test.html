Microphone test

Your browser will ask for permission. Sound stays on your device.

Questions

My mic is not working. What can I try?

Allow microphone access in the address bar, pick the right device in the list, check the mute switch on your headset, and close other apps that use the mic.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

Only if you press record, and then only in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Why is the level low?

Raise the input volume in your system sound settings, or move closer to the mic.

How to test a microphone

Allow microphone access when the browser asks, then speak: a moving level meter means sound is reaching the browser.

If nothing registers

Check that the right input device is selected in the browser prompt and in your system sound settings. Look for a mute switch on the headset or cable. Make sure the site has permission, shown by the icon in the address bar. Close other apps that may be holding the microphone, and try another USB port or re-pair a Bluetooth headset.

Getting good sound

Keep the microphone 10 to 20 cm from your mouth and slightly to the side to avoid popping sounds. Set the input level so normal speech peaks around two-thirds of the meter. Wear headphones on calls to prevent echo.
