    Questions

    How do I convert PES to JEF?

    Drop your PES file in the hoop. JEF is already picked, so press Download.

    Are thread colors kept?

    Yes. JEF stores thread colors, so the colors in your design carry over.

    Is my design uploaded?

    No. The file is converted in your browser.
