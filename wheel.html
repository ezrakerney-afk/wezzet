Spin the wheel

Type your options and spin the wheel to pick one at random.

About spinning a wheel

A spinner wheel picks one entry at random. When every slice is the same size, each has an equal chance: 1 in 8 with eight entries, or 12.5%.

Uses

Choosing who goes first, picking a restaurant, classroom name draws, giveaway winners, chore rotas and workout or writing prompts.

Keeping it fair

Enter each name once. To give someone a better chance, add their entry more than once: two slices out of ten is a 20% chance. For a draw with several winners, remove each winner before spinning again so nobody is picked twice.
