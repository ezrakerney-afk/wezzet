Workspace/Transport tracker

Live transport tracker

Flights, trains and ships around the world, plus airports, ports, stations and railways. Data is fetched live when you search; nothing is stored.

Pick a type and search.

Sources: OpenSky Network (flights), Transitous (trains), aisstream.io (ships worldwide), OpenStreetMap via Overpass (airports, ports, stations, railways, the same data as OpenRailwayMap). API: /api/transport?mode=flights&q=UAL
