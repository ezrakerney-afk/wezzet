Cups to quarts

Convert cups to quarts (assumes water, 1 g per mL), with a table of every other water weight unit.

How to convert cups to quarts

One cup equals 0.25 quarts. To convert, multiply the number of cups by 0.25:

quarts = cups × 0.25

To go the other way, divide by 0.25, or multiply by 4: cups = quarts × 4.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 cups. Multiply 7.5 by 0.25: 7.5 × 0.25 = 1.875 quarts.
