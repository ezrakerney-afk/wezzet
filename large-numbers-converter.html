About large numbers units

Large numbers have different names in different places. In the short scale (US and modern UK) a billion is 10⁹; in the Indian system a lakh is 100,000 and a crore is 10 million.

This converter handles 8 large numbers units: ones, hundreds, thousands, lakhs, millions, crores, billions, trillions. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
