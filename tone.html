Questions

Is it safe for my ears and speakers?

Start with the volume low. Very low and very high tones can be louder than they sound and can harm small speakers.

What can I use it for?

Tuning instruments, testing speakers and headphones, checking your hearing range, or finding a rattle.

Why can I not hear high tones?

Most adults hear up to about 15,000 to 17,000 Hz, and many phone speakers cannot play the very low or very high end.

About tones and frequency

A tone's pitch is its frequency in hertz (Hz), the number of vibrations per second. Doubling the frequency raises the pitch by one octave.

Reference pitches

Concert A is 440 Hz and middle C is about 261.6 Hz. Human hearing runs from roughly 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz when young, and the upper limit falls with age.

Waveforms

A sine wave is a pure tone. Square, sawtooth and triangle waves add harmonics that make the sound buzzier or brighter.

Uses

Tuning instruments, testing speakers and headphones, and checking your hearing range informally.

Protect your hearing and speakers

Start at low volume. High frequencies can be damaging before they seem loud, and very low tones at high volume can harm small speakers.
