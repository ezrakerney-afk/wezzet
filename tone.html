Questions

Is it safe for my ears and speakers?

Start with the volume low. Very low and very high tones can be louder than they sound and can harm small speakers.

What can I use it for?

Tuning instruments, testing speakers and headphones, checking your hearing range, or finding a rattle.

Why can I not hear high tones?

Most adults hear up to about 15,000 to 17,000 Hz, and many phone speakers cannot play the very low or very high end.
