Millivolts to Volts

Convert millivolts to volts, with a table of every other voltage unit.

How to convert millivolts to volts

One millivolt equals 0.001 volts. To convert, multiply the number of millivolts by 0.001:

volts = millivolts × 0.001

To go the other way, divide by 0.001, or multiply by 1,000: millivolts = volts × 1,000.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 millivolts. Multiply 7.5 by 0.001: 7.5 × 0.001 = 0.0075 volts.
