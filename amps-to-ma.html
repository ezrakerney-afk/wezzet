How to convert amps to milliamps

One amp equals 1,000 milliamps. To convert, multiply the number of amps by 1,000:

milliamps = amps × 1,000

To go the other way, divide by 1,000, or multiply by 0.001: amps = milliamps × 0.001.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 amps. Multiply 7.5 by 1,000: 7.5 × 1,000 = 7,500 milliamps.
