Is it truly random?

It uses your browser's cryptographic random generator, so every result is equally likely.

About random choices

A fair random pick gives every option the same chance. Computers produce randomness from a pseudo-random generator, which is unpredictable enough for games and draws, or from a cryptographic generator when the result must be impossible to guess.

Odds

Choosing one of N options gives each a 1 in N chance. Past results do not change future ones: after five heads in a row, the next coin flip is still 50/50.

Fair draws

For a prize draw, list every entry once, pick without replacement so nobody wins twice, and record the result. For random numbers in a range, check whether both end values are included.
