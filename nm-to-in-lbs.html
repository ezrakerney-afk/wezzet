How to convert newton meters to pound-force inches

One newton meter equals 8.850745793 pound-force inches. To convert, multiply the number of newton meters by 8.850745793:

pound-force inches = newton meters × 8.850745793

To go the other way, divide by 8.850745793, or multiply by 0.112984829: newton meters = pound-force inches × 0.112984829.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 newton meters. Multiply 7.5 by 8.850745793: 7.5 × 8.850745793 = 66.38059345 pound-force inches.
