How to convert days to years

One day equals 0.002737907 years. To convert, multiply the number of days by 0.002737907:

years = days × 0.002737907

To go the other way, divide by 0.002737907, or multiply by 365.2425: days = years × 365.2425.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 days. Multiply 7.5 by 0.002737907: 7.5 × 0.002737907 = 0.0205343026 years.
