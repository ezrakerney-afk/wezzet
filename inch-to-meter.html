Inch to meter

Convert inches to meters, with a table of every other length unit.

How to convert inches to meters

One inche equals 0.0254 meters. To convert, multiply the number of inches by 0.0254:

meters = inches × 0.0254

To go the other way, divide by 0.0254, or multiply by 39.37007874: inches = meters × 39.37007874.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 inches. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0254: 7.5 × 0.0254 = 0.1905 meters.
