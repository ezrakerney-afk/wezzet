    Questions

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Conversion happens in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

    Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

    Yes. Drop the HEIC files in and choose JPG.

    What happens to transparency in JPG?

    JPG has no transparency, so clear areas become white. Pick PNG or WebP to keep them.
