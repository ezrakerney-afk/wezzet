    Questions

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Conversion happens in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

    Can I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

    Yes. Drop the HEIC files in and choose JPG.

    What happens to transparency in JPG?

    JPG has no transparency, so clear areas become white. Pick PNG or WebP to keep them.

    About image formats

    JPG is best for photos: small files, opens everywhere, no transparency. PNG is lossless and supports transparency, so it suits screenshots, logos and graphics with text. WebP gives smaller files than both and works in all current browsers. HEIC is what iPhones save, at about half the size of JPG, but many sites and Windows apps cannot open it. GIF is for simple animation, and SVG is for vector logos and icons that stay sharp at any size.

    Camera raw files

    Raw formats such as CR2, NEF, ARW and DNG hold unprocessed sensor data and must be converted before sharing.

    Quality

    Converting to JPG or lossy WebP discards some detail, and each further save loses a little more. Keep the original and convert a copy.
