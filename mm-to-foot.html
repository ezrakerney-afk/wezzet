How to convert millimeters to feet

One millimeter equals 0.0032808399 feet. To convert, multiply the number of millimeters by 0.0032808399:

feet = millimeters × 0.0032808399

To go the other way, divide by 0.0032808399, or multiply by 304.8: millimeters = feet × 304.8.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 millimeters. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0032808399: 7.5 × 0.0032808399 = 0.0246062992 feet.
