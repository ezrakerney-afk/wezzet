Questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. Frames are taken from the video in your browser.

How do I make the GIF smaller?

Use a shorter clip, a smaller width or fewer frames per second. GIFs get big quickly.

Which videos work?

Any video your browser can play. MP4 and WebM work everywhere. iPhone MOV files work in Safari and most other browsers.

About GIFs

GIF is an image format from 1987 that can hold a short animation. It is limited to 256 colors per frame and has no sound, but it plays automatically almost everywhere, which is why it is still used for reactions and short demos.

Keeping the file small

GIFs are inefficient: a few seconds can be several megabytes. Size is driven by the dimensions, the frame rate and the length. Aim for a width of 480 pixels or less, 10 to 15 frames a second and under 6 seconds.

When video is better

An MP4 or WebM of the same clip is typically 5 to 10 times smaller with better color. Use GIF where video is not accepted, such as some chat apps, email and README files.
