Questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. Frames are taken from the video in your browser.

How do I make the GIF smaller?

Use a shorter clip, a smaller width or fewer frames per second. GIFs get big quickly.

Which videos work?

Any video your browser can play. MP4 and WebM work everywhere. iPhone MOV files work in Safari and most other browsers.
