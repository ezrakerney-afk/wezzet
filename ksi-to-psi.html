Convert ksi to psi, with a table of every other pressure unit.

How to convert ksi to psi

One ksi equals 999.9999995 psi. To convert, multiply the number of ksi by 999.9999995:

psi = ksi × 999.9999995

To go the other way, divide by 999.9999995, or multiply by 0.001: ksi = psi × 0.001.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 ksi. Multiply 7.5 by 999.9999995: 7.5 × 999.9999995 = 7,499.999997 psi.
