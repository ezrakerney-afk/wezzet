How to convert milliamp hours to amp hours

One milliamp hour equals 0.001 amp hours. To convert, multiply the number of milliamp hours by 0.001:

amp hours = milliamp hours × 0.001

To go the other way, divide by 0.001, or multiply by 1,000: milliamp hours = amp hours × 1,000.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 milliamp hours. Multiply 7.5 by 0.001: 7.5 × 0.001 = 0.0075 amp hours.
