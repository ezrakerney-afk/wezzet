Convert feet to yards, with a table of every other length unit.

How to convert feet to yards

One feet equals 0.3333333333 yards. To convert, multiply the number of feet by 0.3333333333:

yards = feet × 0.3333333333

To go the other way, divide by 0.3333333333, or multiply by 3: feet = yards × 3.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 feet. Multiply 7.5 by 0.3333333333: 7.5 × 0.3333333333 = 2.5 yards.
