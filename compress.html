    Questions

    How does it hit the size?

    It tries quality levels until the file fits under your target. If even low quality is too big, it makes the image a little smaller and tries again.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. Compression happens in your browser.

    Why JPG or WebP and not PNG?

    PNG has no quality setting, so it can't be squeezed to an exact size. WebP keeps transparency and is usually smaller than JPG.
