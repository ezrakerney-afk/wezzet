About exchange rates

An exchange rate says how much of one currency buys one unit of another. To convert, multiply the amount by the rate: at 1 USD = 0.92 EUR, 250 dollars is 250 × 0.92 = 230 euros.

Mid-market rate

The rate shown by converters is usually the mid-market rate, the midpoint between what banks pay and charge. Banks, card networks and exchange counters add a margin on top, often 1 to 5 percent, so the amount you actually receive is a little lower.

Tips

When paying by card abroad, choose to be charged in the local currency; the conversion offered at the terminal is normally worse. Rates move constantly on weekdays, so check again before a large transfer.
