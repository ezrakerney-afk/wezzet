    Questions

    Is my video uploaded?

    No. The converter runs on your device. The first time, your browser downloads the converter itself (about 32 MB); your files never leave.

    Why is it slow on big videos?

    It runs on your computer's processor inside the browser, which is slower than a desktop app. Short clips and audio are quick.

    About trimming video

    Trimming removes the parts before a start point and after an end point, keeping the section between.

    Timestamps

    Times are written as minutes:seconds, or hours:minutes:seconds. 1:30 means one minute and thirty seconds from the beginning.

    Why cuts can be slightly off

    Video is stored as occasional full frames (keyframes) with the frames in between recorded as changes. Cutting without re-encoding is fast and lossless but can only start on a keyframe, so the clip may begin a moment early. Re-encoding gives a frame-exact cut and takes longer.

    Uses

    Cutting dead time from the start and end of a recording, pulling a highlight out of a long video, and fitting length limits such as 60 or 90 seconds on short-video platforms.
