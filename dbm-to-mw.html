dBm to mW

Convert dbm to milliwatts, with a table of every other power unit.

How to convert dBm to milliwatts

dBm is power on a logarithmic scale relative to one milliwatt. The formula is mW = 10^(dBm ÷ 10).

Worked example

For 20 dBm: 10^(20 ÷ 10) = 10² = 100 mW.

Reference points

0 dBm is 1 mW, 10 dBm is 10 mW, 20 dBm is 100 mW and 30 dBm is 1 W. Going down, −10 dBm is 0.1 mW and −30 dBm is one microwatt.

Every +3 dB roughly doubles the power and every +10 dB multiplies it by ten. Wi-Fi routers transmit at around 20 dBm, and a usable Wi-Fi signal at the receiver is about −70 dBm.
