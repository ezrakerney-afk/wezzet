How to convert megahertz to kilohertz

One megahertz equals 1,000 kilohertz. To convert, multiply the number of megahertz by 1,000:

kilohertz = megahertz × 1,000

To go the other way, divide by 1,000, or multiply by 0.001: megahertz = kilohertz × 0.001.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 megahertz. Multiply 7.5 by 1,000: 7.5 × 1,000 = 7,500 kilohertz.
