Questions

Where is my note saved?

In this browser's local storage on your device. It is never uploaded.

Will my note still be here tomorrow?

Yes, unless you clear site data or use a private window. Download a copy for anything important.

Can I share a note?

Use the paste bin to get a link.

About plain-text notes

Plain text is the simplest and most durable way to keep notes: no formatting, tiny files, and readable by any device or program now and decades from now.

Good uses

Quick drafts, lists, phone numbers during a call, cleaning the formatting off copied text before pasting it elsewhere, and scratch space while working.

Notes kept in a browser

Notes saved by a web page live in that browser's storage on that device. They do not appear on your other devices, and clearing site data or using a private window removes them. Copy anything important into a file or a synced notes app.

Avoid keeping passwords or other secrets in a notepad; use a password manager for those.
