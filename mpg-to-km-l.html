How to convert miles per gallon (us) to kilometers per liter

One miles per gallon (us) equals 0.425143707 kilometers per liter. To convert, multiply the number of miles per gallon (us) by 0.425143707:

kilometers per liter = miles per gallon (us) × 0.425143707

To go the other way, divide by 0.425143707, or multiply by 2.352145836: miles per gallon (us) = kilometers per liter × 2.352145836.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 miles per gallon (us). Multiply 7.5 by 0.425143707: 7.5 × 0.425143707 = 3.188577802 kilometers per liter.
