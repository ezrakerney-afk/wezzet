Questions

Which regex flavor is this?

JavaScript (ECMAScript). Most patterns also work in Python, PHP and Java, but lookbehind and named groups differ slightly.

How do I match across lines?

Use the m flag so ^ and $ match at each line, or the s flag so . also matches newlines.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

About regular expressions

A regular expression (regex) is a pattern that describes text to search for.

Basics

. matches any character, \d a digit, \w a letter, digit or underscore and \s whitespace. [abc] matches one of the listed characters.

How many

* means zero or more, + one or more, ? optional and {2,4} between two and four.

Position and groups

^ and $ match the start and end. Parentheses capture part of a match, and

means or.

Example

^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$ matches a date written like 2025-03-14.

Flags

g finds every match, i ignores case and m makes ^ and $ work on each line.

Quantifiers are greedy and take as much as they can; add ? after one to make it take as little as possible. Syntax differs slightly between languages, so test in the flavor you will use.
