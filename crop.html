Questions

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The image is cropped in your browser and never leaves your device.

Does cropping lower the quality?

No. The crop uses the full resolution of the original. JPG and WebP are saved at high quality.

How do I make a square profile picture?

Pick 1:1, drag the box over the face, then download.

About cropping images

Cropping cuts away the edges of a picture to change its shape or improve the composition.

Common aspect ratios

1:1 is square, used for profile pictures. 4:5 is a portrait feed post. 16:9 is widescreen video, thumbnails and slides, and 9:16 is vertical stories and short videos. 3:2 matches a 6×4 inch photo print and 4:3 most phone cameras.

Composition

Try the rule of thirds: place the subject on one of the lines that divide the frame into thirds, not dead center. Leave space in the direction a person is looking or moving.

Quality

Cropping removes pixels, so a tight crop of a small photo may look soft when enlarged. Start from the largest original you have.
