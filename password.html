Password generator

Questions

Is this safe?

Passwords are made on your device with your browser's secure random generator and are never sent anywhere.

How long should a password be?

16 characters or more with mixed character types is very strong for most accounts.

What makes a strong password

Strength comes from length and randomness. Each added character multiplies the number of possibilities, so a 16-character random password is far stronger than an 8-character one, however many symbols the short one has.

Entropy

A random password drawn from 94 keyboard characters has about 6.5 bits of entropy per character: roughly 52 bits at 8 characters and 105 bits at 16. Above about 80 bits, guessing is not practical.

Passphrases

Four or five random words, such as a diceware phrase, are strong and easier to type and remember.

Good habits

Use a different password for every account, store them in a password manager, and turn on two-factor authentication. Current NIST guidance says to change a password when there is a sign it has leaked, not on a fixed schedule.
