L/100 km to MPG

Convert liters per 100 km to miles per gallon (us), with a table of every other fuel economy unit.

How to convert L/100 km to mpg

Liters per 100 km measures fuel per distance and mpg measures distance per fuel, so the two are inversely related: divide a constant by the value.

mpg (US) = 235.215 ÷ (L/100 km)
mpg (UK) = 282.481 ÷ (L/100 km)

Worked example

A car using 8 L/100 km gets 235.215 ÷ 8 = 29.4 mpg (US), or 282.481 ÷ 8 = 35.3 mpg (UK).

Why two answers

The imperial gallon (4.54609 L) is about 20 percent larger than the US gallon (3.785411784 L), so the same car has a higher mpg figure in the UK.

Because the relationship is inverse, savings are not linear: going from 12 to 10 L/100 km saves more fuel than going from 6 to 5.
