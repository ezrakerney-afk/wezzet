    Questions

    Is my video uploaded?

    No. The converter runs on your device. The first time, your browser downloads the converter itself (about 32 MB); your files never leave.

    Why is it slow on big videos?

    It runs on your computer's processor inside the browser, which is slower than a desktop app. Short clips and audio are quick.

    About FLV and MP4

    What is FLV?

    FLV (Flash Video) was the format of early web video and Adobe Flash Player. Flash was retired in 2020, so browsers no longer play FLV directly.

    What is MP4?

    MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14) is the most widely supported video container. It usually holds H.264 or H.265 video with AAC audio and plays on phones, browsers, TVs and editing software without extra codecs.

    When to convert FLV to MP4

    Choose MP4 when the video has to play everywhere: phones, social networks, messaging apps, slideshows and web pages all accept it.

    How to convert

    Choose your FLV file or drop it on the page, check the output setting, and download the MP4 result. You can add several files at once.
