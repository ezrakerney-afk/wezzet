Questions

What contrast do I need for readable text?

At least 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text, under the WCAG accessibility guidelines.

About picking colors

Screens make colors from red, green and blue light. A color is written as a hex code such as #3366FF, as rgb(51, 102, 255), or as hue, saturation and lightness: hsl(225°, 100%, 60%).

The color wheel

Hue is an angle: 0° is red, 120° green and 240° blue. Complementary colors sit opposite each other (180° apart) and give strong contrast. Analogous colors sit next to each other and feel calm. Triadic colors are 120° apart.

Building a palette

Pick one main color, then make lighter and darker versions by changing only the lightness. Add a single accent color for buttons and links.

Check that text has enough contrast against its background, and remember that colors look different from screen to screen and in print.
