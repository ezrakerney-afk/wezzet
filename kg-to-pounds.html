How to convert kilograms to pounds

One kilogram equals 2.204622622 pounds. To convert, multiply the number of kilograms by 2.204622622:

pounds = kilograms × 2.204622622

To go the other way, divide by 2.204622622, or multiply by 0.45359237: kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 kilograms. Multiply 7.5 by 2.204622622: 7.5 × 2.204622622 = 16.53466966 pounds.
