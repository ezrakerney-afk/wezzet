How to convert stone to pounds

One stone equals 14 pounds. To convert, multiply the number of stone by 14:

pounds = stone × 14

To go the other way, divide by 14, or multiply by 0.0714285714: stone = pounds × 0.0714285714.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 stone. Multiply 7.5 by 14: 7.5 × 14 = 105 pounds.
