How to convert pound-force inches to pound-force feet

One pound-force inche equals 0.0833333333 pound-force feet. To convert, multiply the number of pound-force inches by 0.0833333333:

pound-force feet = pound-force inches × 0.0833333333

To go the other way, divide by 0.0833333333, or multiply by 12: pound-force inches = pound-force feet × 12.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 pound-force inches. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0833333333: 7.5 × 0.0833333333 = 0.6249999999 pound-force feet.
