How to convert RGB to HSV

Scale red, green and blue to 0 to 1 and find the largest (max) and smallest (min).

Value: V = max
Saturation: S = (max − min) ÷ max, or 0 for black
Hue is worked out the same way as in HSL, from which channel is largest.

Worked example

rgb(255, 136, 0) scales to (1, 0.533, 0). V = 1, S = 1 and the hue is 32°, giving hsv(32°, 100%, 100%).

HSV (also called HSB) is the model behind most color pickers: the square shows saturation and value, and the slider shows hue.
