How to convert gibibytes to gigabytes

One gibibyte equals 1.073741824 gigabytes. To convert, multiply the number of gibibytes by 1.073741824:

gigabytes = gibibytes × 1.073741824

To go the other way, divide by 1.073741824, or multiply by 0.9313225746: gibibytes = gigabytes × 0.9313225746.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 gibibytes. Multiply 7.5 by 1.073741824: 7.5 × 1.073741824 = 8.05306368 gigabytes.
