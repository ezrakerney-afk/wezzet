    Questions

    How does it make the PDF smaller?

    Each page is redrawn as a compressed image. This works best on scans and PDFs full of photos.

    Can I still select the text?

    No. Pages become images, so text can't be copied or searched. Keep your original if you need that.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser.
