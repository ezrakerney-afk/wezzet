Magnetic flux converter

Convert between every common unit of magnetic flux. Type a value and pick units.

About magnetic flux units

Magnetic flux is the total magnetic field passing through an area, in webers. One weber is 100 million maxwells.

This converter handles 7 magnetic flux units: webers, milliwebers, microwebers, maxwells, volt seconds, unit poles, kilolines. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
