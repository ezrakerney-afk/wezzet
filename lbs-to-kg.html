Lbs to kg

Convert pounds to kilograms (assumes water, 1 g per mL), with a table of every other water weight unit.

How to convert pounds to kilograms

One pound equals 0.45359237 kilograms. To convert, multiply the number of pounds by 0.45359237:

kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237

To go the other way, divide by 0.45359237, or multiply by 2.204622622: pounds = kilograms × 2.204622622.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 pounds. Multiply 7.5 by 0.45359237: 7.5 × 0.45359237 = 3.401942775 kilograms.
