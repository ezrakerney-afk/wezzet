Roman numerals

Convert numbers to Roman numerals and back, from 1 to 3999.

How Roman numerals work

Roman numerals use seven letters: I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50, C = 100, D = 500 and M = 1,000.

Rules

Write the largest values first and add them: VIII is 5 + 3 = 8. A smaller letter before a larger one is subtracted: IV is 4, IX is 9, XL is 40, XC is 90, CD is 400 and CM is 900. A letter is repeated at most three times in a row.

Worked example

2024 is 2000 + 20 + 4 = MM + XX + IV = MMXXIV. 1994 is M + CM + XC + IV = MCMXCIV.

Limits

The standard system has no zero and stops at 3,999 (MMMCMXCIX). Larger numbers were written with a bar over a letter to multiply it by 1,000.
