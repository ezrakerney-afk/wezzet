How to convert kilograms per square centimeter to psi

One kilograms per square centimeter equals 14.22334331 psi. To convert, multiply the number of kilograms per square centimeter by 14.22334331:

psi = kilograms per square centimeter × 14.22334331

To go the other way, divide by 14.22334331, or multiply by 0.070306958: kilograms per square centimeter = psi × 0.070306958.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 kilograms per square centimeter. Multiply 7.5 by 14.22334331: 7.5 × 14.22334331 = 106.6750748 psi.
