How to convert kilograms to stone

One kilogram equals 0.1574730444 stone. To convert, multiply the number of kilograms by 0.1574730444:

stone = kilograms × 0.1574730444

To go the other way, divide by 0.1574730444, or multiply by 6.35029318: kilograms = stone × 6.35029318.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 kilograms. Multiply 7.5 by 0.1574730444: 7.5 × 0.1574730444 = 1.181047833 stone.
