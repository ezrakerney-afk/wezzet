How to convert Rankine to Fahrenheit

The formula is °F = °R − 459.67.

Worked example

Take 25 °R. Putting it through the formula gives -434.67 °F.

About the scales

Celsius sets 0 at the freezing point of water and 100 at its boiling point. Fahrenheit puts those at 32 and 212, so one Celsius degree is 1.8 Fahrenheit degrees. Kelvin uses Celsius-sized steps but starts at absolute zero (−273.15 °C), and Rankine does the same with Fahrenheit-sized steps. Kelvin and Rankine have no negative values, which is why science and engineering formulas use them.
