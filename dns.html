Questions

Where do the answers come from?

From Cloudflare public DNS (1.1.1.1), so you see what the internet sees right now, not your local cache.

Can I do a reverse lookup?

Yes. Type an IPv4 address and you get its PTR record (the host name).

What is TTL?

Time to live: how many seconds resolvers may cache the record before asking again.

Is there an API?

Yes: wezzet.com/api/dns?name=example.com&type=MX returns plain text, or JSON with &fmt=json.

About DNS records

DNS (Domain Name System) turns a domain name into the addresses and settings computers need.

Record types

A points a name to an IPv4 address and AAAA to an IPv6 address. CNAME makes one name an alias of another. MX lists the mail servers for a domain, with lower numbers tried first. TXT holds text such as SPF and DKIM email settings and site verification codes. NS names the servers that are authoritative for the domain.

TTL and propagation

Each record has a time to live (TTL) in seconds that tells resolvers how long to cache it. After a change, some users keep seeing the old value until their cache expires, which is why updates can take minutes to a day or two to reach everyone.
