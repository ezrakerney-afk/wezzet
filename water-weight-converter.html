Water weight converter

Convert between volume and weight for water: mL to grams, cups to pounds, gallons to kg and more.

About water weight units

Water links volume and weight simply: one liter of pure water weighs one kilogram at about 4 °C, and a US gallon weighs about 8.34 pounds.

This converter handles 22 water weight units: milligrams, grams, kilograms, ounces, pounds, us tons, metric tons, microliters, drops, milliliters, cubic centimeters, liters, teaspoons, tablespoons, fluid ounces, cups, pints, quarts, gallons, cubic feet, cubic meters, cubic yards. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
