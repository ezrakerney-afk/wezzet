Questions

My camera shows a black screen. What can I try?

Allow camera access in the address bar, close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, Teams, OBS), and check for a privacy shutter or a keyboard camera key.

Is my video recorded or uploaded?

No. The picture is only shown on this page. Photos are saved straight to your device.

Why is the resolution lower than my camera's?

The page asks for up to 4K and the browser picks the best mode the camera offers. Lighting and USB bandwidth can lower it.
