Questions

My camera shows a black screen. What can I try?

Allow camera access in the address bar, close other apps that use the camera (Zoom, Teams, OBS), and check for a privacy shutter or a keyboard camera key.

Is my video recorded or uploaded?

No. The picture is only shown on this page. Photos are saved straight to your device.

Why is the resolution lower than my camera's?

The page asks for up to 4K and the browser picks the best mode the camera offers. Lighting and USB bandwidth can lower it.

How to test a webcam

Allow camera access when the browser asks. If you see yourself, the camera works.

If the picture is black or missing

Check for a privacy shutter or a camera key on the keyboard. Close other apps that may be using the camera, such as Zoom, Teams or FaceTime, since many cameras work with one app at a time. Check the site's permission by the icon in the address bar, and the camera privacy setting in your operating system. If you have several cameras, pick the right one in the prompt.

Looking better on calls

Face a window or lamp so the light falls on your face, not behind you. Raise the camera to eye level. Wipe the lens. A 720p picture at 30 frames a second is enough for most video calls.
