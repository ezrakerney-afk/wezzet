Cents to dollars

Convert cents to dollars, with a table of every other us coins unit.

How to convert cents to dollars

One cent equals 0.01 dollars. To convert, multiply the number of cents by 0.01:

dollars = cents × 0.01

To go the other way, divide by 0.01, or multiply by 100: cents = dollars × 100.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 cents. Multiply 7.5 by 0.01: 7.5 × 0.01 = 0.075 dollars.
