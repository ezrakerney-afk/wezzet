Questions

What's in the zip?

favicon.ico with 16, 32 and 48 px sizes inside, a 32 px PNG, a 180 px Apple touch icon, and 192 and 512 px icons for Android and web apps.

What image works best?

A square PNG or SVG at least 512 px wide. Other shapes are centered with clear space around them.

About favicons

A favicon is the small icon browsers show in tabs, bookmarks and history.

Sizes to provide

A favicon.ico containing 16, 32 and 48 pixel versions covers desktop browsers. Add a 180 × 180 PNG as the apple-touch-icon for iPhone and iPad home screens, and 192 and 512 pixel PNGs in a web app manifest for Android. An SVG favicon stays sharp at any size and can adapt to dark mode in browsers that support it.

Design tips

A favicon is tiny, so use a simple shape or a single letter with strong contrast. Detailed logos turn to mush at 16 pixels.

Why the old icon still shows

Browsers cache favicons aggressively. After changing yours, do a hard refresh or open the icon's address directly to see the new one.
