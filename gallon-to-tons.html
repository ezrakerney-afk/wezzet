How to convert gallons to us tons

One gallon equals 0.0041727022 us tons. To convert, multiply the number of gallons by 0.0041727022:

us tons = gallons × 0.0041727022

To go the other way, divide by 0.0041727022, or multiply by 239.6528546: gallons = us tons × 239.6528546.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 gallons. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0041727022: 7.5 × 0.0041727022 = 0.0312952667 us tons.
