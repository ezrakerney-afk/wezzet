GW to Watts

Convert gigawatts to watts, with a table of every other power unit.

How to convert gigawatts to watts

One gigawatt equals 1,000,000,000 watts. To convert, multiply the number of gigawatts by 1,000,000,000:

watts = gigawatts × 1,000,000,000

To go the other way, divide by 1,000,000,000, or multiply by 1e-9: gigawatts = watts × 1e-9.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 gigawatts. Multiply 7.5 by 1,000,000,000: 7.5 × 1,000,000,000 = 7,500,000,000 watts.
