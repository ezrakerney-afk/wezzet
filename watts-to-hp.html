How to convert watts to horsepower

One watt equals 0.0013410221 horsepower. To convert, multiply the number of watts by 0.0013410221:

horsepower = watts × 0.0013410221

To go the other way, divide by 0.0013410221, or multiply by 745.6998716: watts = horsepower × 745.6998716.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 watts. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0013410221: 7.5 × 0.0013410221 = 0.0100576657 horsepower.
