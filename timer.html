    Will the alarm sound if I switch tabs?

    Yes, as long as this tab stays open and your sound is on.

    About timers

    A timer counts down from a set length and alerts you at zero; a stopwatch counts up from zero.

    Common timings

    Soft-boiled eggs take about 6 minutes and hard-boiled 10 to 12. Black tea steeps for 3 to 5 minutes and green tea for 2 to 3. Dentists recommend brushing for 2 minutes.

    The Pomodoro technique

    Work for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break, and after four rounds take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes. Short fixed sessions make large tasks easier to start.

    Keep in mind

    Browsers slow down background tabs to save power. The time is still calculated correctly, but leave the tab open and your sound on so you hear the alert.
