Convert ares to hectares, with a table of every other area unit.

How to convert ares to hectares

One are equals 0.01 hectares. To convert, multiply the number of ares by 0.01:

hectares = ares × 0.01

To go the other way, divide by 0.01, or multiply by 100: ares = hectares × 100.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 ares. Multiply 7.5 by 0.01: 7.5 × 0.01 = 0.075 hectares.
