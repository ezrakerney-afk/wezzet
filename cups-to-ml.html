How to convert cups to milliliters

One cup equals 236.5882365 milliliters. To convert, multiply the number of cups by 236.5882365:

milliliters = cups × 236.5882365

To go the other way, divide by 236.5882365, or multiply by 0.0042267528: cups = milliliters × 0.0042267528.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 cups. Multiply 7.5 by 236.5882365: 7.5 × 236.5882365 = 1,774.411774 milliliters.
