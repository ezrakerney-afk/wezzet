How to convert weeks to months

One week equals 0.2299841886 months. To convert, multiply the number of weeks by 0.2299841886:

months = weeks × 0.2299841886

To go the other way, divide by 0.2299841886, or multiply by 4.348125: weeks = months × 4.348125.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 weeks. Multiply 7.5 by 0.2299841886: 7.5 × 0.2299841886 = 1.724881414 months.
