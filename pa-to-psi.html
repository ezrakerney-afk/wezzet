How to convert pascals to psi

One pascal equals 0.0001450377 psi. To convert, multiply the number of pascals by 0.0001450377:

psi = pascals × 0.0001450377

To go the other way, divide by 0.0001450377, or multiply by 6,894.757293: pascals = psi × 6,894.757293.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 pascals. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0001450377: 7.5 × 0.0001450377 = 0.001087783 psi.
