Questions

How do I convert STL to OBJ?

Drop your STL file in the box. OBJ is already picked, so press Download.

Will the OBJ be smaller?

Usually. Shared corners are stored once, so OBJ files are often smaller than text STL.

Is my model uploaded?

No. The file is converted in your browser.
