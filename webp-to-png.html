    Questions

    Does PNG keep transparency?

    Yes. Clear areas in your WebP stay clear in the PNG.

    Why are WebP files hard to use?

    Some older apps and editors can't open WebP. PNG works almost everywhere.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Conversion happens in your browser.

    About WebP and PNG

    What is WebP?

    WebP is Google's image format for the web. It supports lossy and lossless compression, transparency and animation, and is typically 25 to 35 percent smaller than JPG at similar quality.

    What is PNG?

    PNG is a lossless image format with full transparency support. It keeps sharp edges and text perfectly, but photos saved as PNG are much larger than JPG.

    When to convert WebP to PNG

    Choose PNG for screenshots, logos, graphics with text and anything that needs a transparent background.

    How to convert

    Choose your WebP file or drop it on the page, check the output setting, and download the PNG result. You can add several files at once.
