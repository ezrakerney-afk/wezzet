How to convert meters per second to kilometers per hour

One meters per second equals 3.6 kilometers per hour. To convert, multiply the number of meters per second by 3.6:

kilometers per hour = meters per second × 3.6

To go the other way, divide by 3.6, or multiply by 0.2777777778: meters per second = kilometers per hour × 0.2777777778.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 meters per second. Multiply 7.5 by 3.6: 7.5 × 3.6 = 27 kilometers per hour.
