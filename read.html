Questions

What does it remove?

Scripts, styles, menus, sidebars, footers and hidden parts. You get the title, headings, text, lists, tables and links.

Does it work on every site?

Most public pages work. Pages behind a login or built only with JavaScript may come back empty. For PDFs, use PDF to text.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. Open wezzet.com/api/read?url=example.com. Long pages come in parts with a link to the next part. Limit: 60 pages an hour.
