Web page to text

Questions

What does it remove?

Scripts, styles, menus, sidebars, footers and hidden parts. You get the title, headings, text, lists, tables and links.

Does it work on every site?

Most public pages work. Pages behind a login or built only with JavaScript may come back empty. For PDFs, use PDF to text.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. Open wezzet.com/api/read?url=example.com. Long pages come in parts with a link to the next part. Limit: 60 pages an hour.

About reading aloud

Text to speech turns written text into spoken audio with a synthetic voice. Browsers include speech voices, and the ones available depend on your device and operating system.

Why listen

Proofreading: your ear catches missing words and awkward sentences that your eye skips. Accessibility for people with low vision or dyslexia. Getting through long articles while doing something else.

Speed

Normal speech is about 150 words a minute. Many people can follow 1.5 to 2 times that speed with a little practice.

Better results

Clean up the text first: remove page numbers, footnote marks and line breaks in the middle of sentences, which make the voice pause in odd places.
