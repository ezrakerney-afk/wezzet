How to convert kilowatts to horsepower

One kilowatt equals 1.34102209 horsepower. To convert, multiply the number of kilowatts by 1.34102209:

horsepower = kilowatts × 1.34102209

To go the other way, divide by 1.34102209, or multiply by 0.7456998716: kilowatts = horsepower × 0.7456998716.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 kilowatts. Multiply 7.5 by 1.34102209: 7.5 × 1.34102209 = 10.05766567 horsepower.
