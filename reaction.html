About reaction time

Reaction time is the delay between seeing a signal and responding to it. For a simple visual test, most adults average 200 to 270 milliseconds; responses to sound are faster, around 150 to 180 ms.

What affects it

Tiredness, alcohol, distraction and age slow it down; practice, alertness and caffeine can speed it up slightly.

Your equipment matters

Screens, mice and wireless connections add delay, often 20 to 80 ms in total, so scores are not comparable between devices. A phone touchscreen usually reads slower than a gaming mouse and monitor.

Getting a fair result

Take several attempts and use the average, since single tries vary a lot. In athletics, a start faster than 100 ms after the gun counts as a false start because no one can react that quickly.
