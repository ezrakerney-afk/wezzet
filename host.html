        Who can see my page?

        Anyone with the link. Pages also show in the Explore list unless you turn that off when you publish or in My pages.

        What can I upload?

        HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, sounds, fonts, JSON and WebAssembly, up to 2 MB per page and 5 pages per account. Name the start page index.html, or upload a single HTML file.

        Can I make a page without any files?

        Yes. Open the Editor, pick a template or write your own HTML, watch the live preview, then publish.

        Why can't my page save data?

        Hosted pages run in a sandbox, cut off from the rest of Wezzet, so your visitors' accounts stay safe. That also blocks cookies and localStorage. Password fields aren't allowed either.

        How do I update or delete it?

        Go to My pages. Edit opens the page in the editor, Replace uploads new files under the same name, and Delete removes it.

        How are views counted?

        Each time someone opens your page link, it counts one view. Bots and previews are skipped.
