        Page access

          What does my AI need?

          An AI with web/API access or an MCP connection to https://wezzet.com/mcp. The tools are host_publish and host_source. A chat that cannot call tools can still produce files for you to upload.

          Free accounts: one page, 2 MB total, 200 files. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript only. No server backend, cookies or localStorage.

          Help

          • Upload or writeDrop in a folder, or build a page in the editor with live preview.
          • Your own linkPick a name and share wezzet.com/host/your-name.
          • Safe by designEvery page runs in an isolated sandbox. Report a page from its Explore card.
          Who can see my page?

          Anyone with the link. Pages also show in the Explore list unless you turn that off when you publish or in My pages.

          What can I upload?

          HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images, sounds, fonts, JSON and WebAssembly, up to 2 MB per page. Free accounts get 1 page, and Wezzet Pro gets 5. Name the start page index.html, or upload a single HTML file.

          Can I make a page without any files?

          Yes. Open the Editor, pick a template or write your own HTML, watch the live preview, then publish.

          Why can't my page save data?

          Hosted pages run in a sandbox, cut off from the rest of Wezzet, so your visitors' accounts stay safe. That also blocks cookies and localStorage. Password fields aren't allowed either.

          How do I update or delete it?

          Go to My pages. Edit opens the page in the editor, Replace uploads new files under the same name, and Delete removes it.

          How are views counted?

          Each time someone opens your page link, it counts one view. Bots and previews are skipped.
