Convert between every common unit of energy. Type a value and pick units.

About energy units

Energy is the capacity to do work. The SI unit is the joule; electricity is billed in kilowatt-hours (3.6 MJ), food is labeled in kilocalories (4.184 kJ) and gas heating often uses BTU.

This converter handles 14 energy units: joules, kilojoules, megajoules, calories, kilocalories, watt hours, kilowatt hours, btu, therms, electron volts, kiloelectron volts, megaelectron volts, gigaelectron volts, foot-pounds. Type a value, pick the unit you have and the unit you want, and the table shows the same amount in every other unit at once.
