    Questions

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The cutout model downloads to your browser once and runs on your device.

    Which option should I pick?

    A person is quick and great for portraits and selfies. Anything else handles products, pets and objects, but the model is bigger and takes longer the first time.

    Why is the first one slow?

    The model has to download first, about 26 MB for people or 115 MB for anything else. After that it's saved by your browser.
