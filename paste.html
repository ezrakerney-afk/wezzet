Questions

Who can see my paste?

Anyone with the link. Links are random and not listed anywhere, but don't save passwords or private details.

How long is it kept?

For the time you pick, counted from the last edit. You can edit or delete it from the same browser you saved it in.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. Open wezzet.com/api/paste/new?text=hello to save and wezzet.com/api/paste/ID to read. Full guide: tools.txt.
