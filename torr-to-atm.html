How to convert torr to atmospheres

One torr equals 0.0013157895 atmospheres. To convert, multiply the number of torr by 0.0013157895:

atmospheres = torr × 0.0013157895

To go the other way, divide by 0.0013157895, or multiply by 760: torr = atmospheres × 760.

Worked example

Say you have 7.5 torr. Multiply 7.5 by 0.0013157895: 7.5 × 0.0013157895 = 0.0098684211 atmospheres.
